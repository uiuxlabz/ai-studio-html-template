The studio

We build intelligence that earns its place.

KORVA started in 2020 as a research group that got tired of demos that never shipped. Today we're a studio of engineers, researchers, and designers building applied AI for teams that measure.

From papers to production

We publish less than we ship. The work that matters to us is the model running quietly in your infrastructure at 3am, holding up under load, and improving every quarter because we built the eval loop to prove it.

That means we say no often. If a general-purpose model already solves your problem, we'll tell you to use it. We earn our keep on the messy, domain-specific, regulated edges where off-the-shelf falls apart.

We're small by design — senior people, few layers, direct lines to the people doing the work.

What we value

Four things we won't compromise

🎯

Outcome over output

A model that ships and measurably helps beats a clever one that doesn't. We optimize for the metric, not the headline.

🔍

You own it

Weights, code, eval sets — handed over. No black boxes, no lock-in, no mystery bills.

🛡️

Honest about limits

We tell you when AI isn't the right tool. The fastest way to lose trust is to oversell it.

📈

Measure everything

Every system ships with an evaluation suite. If we can't measure it, we don't claim it.

Questions

Things people ask

Do we own the model we build?
Yes. Unless we explicitly agree otherwise, the weights, training code, and evaluation sets are yours. We can host it, but the IP is your property from day one.
Can you work in our cloud or on-prem?
Most of our production work runs in the client's environment. We support AWS, GCP, Azure, and air-gapped on-prem deployments.
How do you price a project?
Fixed-scope for well-defined builds, monthly retainer for ongoing research. We'll give you a straight estimate after a short scoping call.
What if AI isn't the right tool?
We'll tell you. Roughly a third of inbound conversations end with us recommending a simpler, cheaper solution — and we're fine with that.

Want to build with us?

Tell us the decision you're trying to automate. The first call is free and honest.
