Our Story

Built by engineers, for engineers

KORVA was founded in 2018 by a team of ML engineers who were tired of watching AI projects fail in production. The industry was full of impressive demos and broken deployments.

We built KORVA on one principle: if it doesn't work in production, it doesn't work at all. Every project starts with a success metric. Every model is validated against real-world data. Every deployment includes monitoring, drift detection, and retraining pipelines.

Today, we maintain 127 deployed models across 34 enterprise clients — with a 99% uptime SLA and an average time-to-production of 8 weeks.
